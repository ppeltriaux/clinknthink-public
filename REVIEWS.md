## Reply guidelines

- Warm and brief — a few sentences, never a wall of text.
- Thank the reviewer by name where they used one.
- Name the mode they mention (see [How to Play](HOW-TO-PLAY.md)) and suggest one
  natural next step.
- Reinforce the promise: no ads, no tracking, works offline.
- Never ask for a rating change; point issues at
  [Support](https://peltriaux.com/clinknthink/support.html).
