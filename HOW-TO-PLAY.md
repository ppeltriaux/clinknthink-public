# Clink & Think — How to Play

Two gestures is all a child needs. Everything else is discovery.

No reading is required in the app — this guide is for the grown-up sitting alongside.

🌐 Web version: https://peltriaux.com/clinknthink/how-to-play.html

## The whole game, in three moves

Every mode is built on the same simple handling, so a child who learns one mode
already knows them all.

1. **Drag a marble in.** Press a marble in the tray at the bottom and drag it over
   a glass tube. Let go — it drops and stacks up from the bottom with a clink.
2. **Tap a tube to pour.** Tap any filled tube and it tips over and empties. That's
   the undo — nothing is ever lost or wrong, just pour it out and try again.
3. **Match, and celebrate.** There's no button to press and nothing to submit. The
   moment the tubes match, confetti, sparkles and a chime arrive on their own.

The six marble colours are **red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple**. Every
colour also carries its own shape (▲ ■ ★ ● ◆ ✚), so the game plays the same for
colour-blind children.

## The nine modes, step by step

Free Play, Learn, Pattern and the first Adventure world are free. The rest come
with the one-time Full Unlock.

### 🎨 Free Play — *free*
A sandbox with no goal, no card and no way to lose. The best place to start.

1. Drag any marble from the tray into any tube.
2. Fill the tubes however they like — stripes, one colour, a jumble.
3. **Tap a tube** to pour it out and start again.

> **Grown-up tip:** spend the first session here. Once the drag-and-pour is second
> nature, every other mode explains itself.

### 🎓 Learn — *free*
A friendly voice asks for a number of a colour in a particular tube — counting,
colour names and left / middle / right, all in one.

1. Listen: *"Put **three red** marbles in the **middle** tube."* The prompt is
   spoken and shown.
2. Drag that many of that colour into that tube.
3. Get it right and you're praised, then the next prompt arrives. Miscount? Pour
   and retry — nothing is marked wrong.

> **Six languages:** the voice speaks English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil),
> Italian or German — it follows the device, or pick one in Settings.

### 🔵 Pattern Play — *free*
A string of beads with the end missing. Spot the repeat and keep it going — the
classic pre-maths patterning skill.

1. Look at the row: something like **red · blue · red · blue · …**
2. The last few slots are empty. Drag the colour the pattern calls for into each blank.
3. Fill every blank correctly and a new pattern is served.

> **Grown-up tip:** say the pattern out loud together — "red, blue, red, blue, so
> what comes next?" Hearing the rhythm is how the rule clicks.

### 🗺️ Adventure — *world 1 free*
A map of nine little worlds — a Playroom, a Truck Yard, a Castle Keep, a Rescue Bay
and more — strung together as 56 short levels.

1. Tap the glowing tile on the map. That's the next level.
2. Play the puzzle it hands you — each world is built around one mode.
3. Win, and the path lights up to the next tile. Stickers arrive along the way.
4. Clear a whole world and it hands over its **marble theme** — fruit, animals,
   dinosaurs, space, cars, trucks and more — usable everywhere in the game.

> **Themes are earned, not bought:** playing the Adventure is how a child collects
> the marble sets. There is no way to lose progress on the map.

### 🃏 Challenge — *Full Unlock*
A recipe card appears above the tubes. Build exactly what it shows.

1. Read the card: each column of dots is one tube, bottom marble at the bottom.
2. Drag marbles in so every tube matches its column — right colours, right order.
3. Wrong stack? Tap that tube to pour it out and rebuild it.
4. The instant all tubes match, the celebration fires by itself.

### 🧺 Sort — *Full Unlock*
The tubes start scrambled. Tidy them until each tube holds a single colour. This is
the one mode where marbles move *between* tubes.

1. Drag the **top** marble off a tube.
2. Drop it on an **empty** tube, or on a tube whose top marble is the **same colour**.
3. Any other drop simply bounces back — an illegal move can't be made by accident.
4. Keep going until every tube is one clean colour.

> **Undo** takes back the last move and **Hint** shows a good next one — both sit
> under the tubes, use them freely. The very first time a child opens Sort, the game
> plays one move for them so they can see how it works.

### 🧠 Memory — *Full Unlock*
Challenge, but the card hides itself. Look, remember, then build it blind.

1. The card is shown for a moment — the voice says *"Look and remember!"* — then a
   cover slides over it and it says *"Now build it!"*
2. Build what you remember with the marbles.
3. Forgotten it? **Tap the covered card** for a two-second peek (*"Quick look!"*). Peek
   as often as you like — there's no penalty and nothing is counted.
4. Filled every tube but it doesn't match? A gentle *"Not quite! Take another look."*
   — said once, never nagging. Tap a tube to pour it out and try again.
5. Match the hidden card and you hear *"You remembered!"* with the celebration.

Because the card disappears, Memory is the one mode where a child who can't read yet
needs telling what the game is waiting for — so the voice narrates the whole turn, in
whichever of the six languages you picked.

### 🧪 Color-Mix — *Full Unlock*
A beaker and a target colour. Work out which colours make it — real subtractive
colour mixing, the first bit of proper STEM in the game.

1. Look at the target swatch — say, **orange**.
2. Drag the ingredient colours from the tray into the beaker.
3. Two primaries make a secondary (**red + yellow = orange**). A primary plus a
   neighbouring secondary makes a tertiary. All three primaries make **brown**.
4. Hit the target and the beaker fills with the colour they just invented.

> **It grows quietly:** the tray starts with just the three primaries, and widens as
> the child succeeds — up to ten makeable colours. Easy difficulty stays on
> secondaries and never ramps.

### 🏠 Decorate the Playroom — *Full Unlock*
Not a puzzle — a reward. Every sticker earned in the Sticker Book can be placed in a
room of their own.

1. Open the Playroom from the home screen.
2. Pick a sticker you've earned and drag it anywhere in the room.
3. Move it, or take it back out, whenever you like. It stays exactly as they left it.

## Picking the right difficulty

Set it on the home screen, above the modes. It's not a score — pick the one that
keeps the play calm.

| Preset | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Easy** | 3 tubes, 3 colours, and the card shows *counts* rather than exact stacks. The right starting point for a 4-year-old — and Color-Mix stays on the simple secondaries here. |
| **Medium** | 4 tubes, 4 colours, exact colour order on the card. Adds the tertiary colours to Color-Mix. |
| **Hard** | 5 tubes, 5 colours, and brown joins Color-Mix. Comfortable for a confident 6-year-old. |

## For the grown-up

The things a child never has to think about — all behind a parental gate.

- **Parental gate** — a quick multiplication question guards Settings and the store,
  so nothing important can be tapped by accident. You can switch it to Face ID /
  Touch ID instead; the question stays as the fallback.
- **Coming back** — leave mid-puzzle and the game offers **Continue** when you return,
  or a fresh start. In-game, swipe from the left edge to go back to the home screen.
- **Play time** — optional, off by default: set a gentle daily limit (15–60 min). It
  never interrupts a puzzle — the child finishes, then a calm wind-down appears. No
  clock or countdown is ever shown to the child, and running out never shows the
  paywall. You can grant more time behind the gate.
- **Child's Progress** — a read-only dashboard: what they've played, what they've
  earned, and a "This week" summary of minutes per day and which skills got practised.
- **Daily treat** — a sticker and a gentle streak for coming back — never punishing,
  never nagging, and missing a day costs nothing.
- **Sound & voice** — toggle the sound effects and the spoken voice separately, and
  pick the app's language, in Settings. The voice names colours, counts aloud in Learn
  and narrates Memory, in six languages.
- **Across devices** — progress, stickers and earned themes sync over iCloud between
  your child's own iPhone and iPad. No account, no sign-up, no data collected.
